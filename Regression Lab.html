How OLS gets the numbers

Live values for the points on the chart.

  • R² is the share of the variation in the outcome that the line accounts for. It runs from 0 to 1.
  • Standard error measures how much the slope would bounce around from one sample to another.
  • p-value is the chance of seeing a slope this far from zero if the true slope were zero.

A regression shows association. Whether the slope is a causal effect depends on what else moves with the explanatory variable, which is why economists add controls, instruments and experiments. Datasets here are generated from made-up relationships so the numbers behave predictably.
